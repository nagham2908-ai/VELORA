How It's Made

01

Cut

Plastic canvas sheets are cut to pattern for the size and silhouette you've chosen — mini clutch, standard clutch, crossbody, or tote.

02

Weave

Cord is threaded row by row through the canvas grid in your chosen weave — classic, herringbone, diamond chevron, or checker mosaic.

03

Reinforce

Panels are joined and reinforced at every stress point, so the bag holds its structure without ever feeling stiff.

04

Finish

Hardware, straps, and thread finish are added last, by hand, before your bag is packed and shipped straight from the studio.

Materials & Care

Plastic Canvas Base

A rigid, gridded mesh that holds structure without stiff linings or internal frames — lightweight, water-resistant, and shape-stable.

Woven Cord

Durable polyester cord threaded by hand through every cell of the canvas — colorfast, flexible, and built to resist fraying.

Hardware & Straps

Turn-locks, twist-locks, and snaps in matte, gold, silver, or brass finishes; straps in chain, braided cord, or adjustable crossbody webbing.

Care: wipe clean with a damp cloth. Avoid prolonged direct sunlight to preserve color saturation. Store flat or upright — never folded — to protect the weave.
